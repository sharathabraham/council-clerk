This section works differently from the rest of the site.

These boards' minutes are scanned court-reporter transcripts, not the structured documents the Mayor & Council tracker parses automatically — so this list is a small, hand-read, one-time snapshot of the most recent Zoning and Planning Board meeting available, not an auto-updating feed. Both boards' own minutes were already over a year old as of when this was written (Zoning: June 2025, Planning: May 2025) — that lag is the Borough's own publishing pace, not something on our end.

Zoning Board of Adjustment — June 25, 2025

Decides requests to deviate from the Borough's normal zoning rules (setbacks, height, lot coverage) for a specific property.

Building Coverage

Thomas & Tina Montone — 158 Hillman Drive

Variance for building coverage (Block 1307, Lot 12).

Approved as written — Members Baglieri and Castro-Place abstained.
Height & Lot Coverage

MBP Hoist & Crane — 107 Kipp Avenue

Variance for building height, lot coverage, and a vestibule basement entrance. The applicant reduced the building's height to 29.99 feet and eliminated livable attic space to bring the plan into compliance before approval.

Approved with stipulations — Member Castro-Place abstained.
Setback

Tara & Joseph Abalsamo — 129 Elmwood Drive

Variance to place a 15-foot round pool 7 feet into the right-of-way setback, due to a 10-foot drainage easement at the rear of the property (Block 202, Lot 25). Condition: if the Borough ever needs to access the easement, the pool and fencing must be removed.

Approved unanimously, with conditions.
