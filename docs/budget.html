Where your property tax dollar goes

Total tax levy, by year

The total dollar amount raised through property taxes across all entities (municipal, school, and county) each year. 2017 is missing because the Borough hasn't posted a filing that reports it as a finalized year.

Total tax rate, by year

The combined tax rate (per $100 of assessed value) across all entities. This is what actually determines your bill alongside your home's assessment.

Source: the Borough's own "User Friendly Budget" filings, a standardized form required by the NJ Division of Local Government Services. Figures shown are the finalized "Calendar Year" actuals from each filing, not budget estimates, except where noted.
