📌 Good to know

Attending or watching a meeting

  • Meetings are held at the Municipal Building, 182 Market Street, typically at 7:00 PM.
  • Agendas are usually posted before the meeting — so you can see what's coming up, not just what already happened.
  • Every meeting we have a recording for has the video embedded right on its meeting page.
  • Not sure what "consent agenda" or "first/second reading" mean? See How It Works.
